About this wallet

Your keys are created and encrypted in this browser (PBKDF2-SHA512, 600,000 rounds, AES-256-GCM). Nothing secret is ever sent anywhere.

Every transaction is signed with ML-DSA-87 (NIST FIPS 204), the post-quantum signature Final Layer requires. A transfer carries 7.2 KB of public key and signature, which is why the fee is a little higher than on classical chains.

Wallets saved here exist only in this browser on this site. Back up your recovery phrase.
